How does stress affect your stomach?

Short answer. Your brain and gut are in constant contact, and stress can change how quickly food moves, how sensitive the gut feels and how much it secretes. It is linked with several gut conditions, including reflux and irritable bowel syndrome. So the same meal can feel different on a tense day.

What the review describes

  • Changes in how the gut moves.
  • The gut feels more: normal stretching can feel uncomfortable.
  • Changes in what the gut secretes, and in the gut's bacteria.

It also notes that care for irritable bowel syndrome often includes behavioural and psychological treatment alongside food and medicine.

Why this ruins food diaries

Stress hides behind food. On a hard day you eat later, faster, and different things. If you only log food, the takeaway gets the blame that may belong to the day. That is why one number for stress, every day, is worth more than another list of ingredients.

What it can't show

A pattern like this says the two go together in your days. It doesn't say which came first: a sore stomach is stressful too.

Source

  • Konturek PC, Brzozowski T, Konturek SJ. Stress and the gut. J Physiol Pharmacol, 2011
