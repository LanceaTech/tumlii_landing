How long should you wait before lying down after eating?

Short answer. Many reflux guides suggest about 3 hours between dinner and bed. In a study of 147 people with reflux disease and 294 without, going to bed within 3 hours of dinner went with much higher odds of being in the reflux group than waiting 4 hours or more.

What the study found

Researchers in Osaka compared 147 people with reflux disease with 294 people of the same age and sex who had had no heartburn or regurgitation for a year. Everyone said how long after finishing dinner they usually went to bed. After allowing for smoking, drinking and body mass index, the odds of reflux disease were about 7 times higher (7.45, with a range of 3.4 to 16.4) in people who went to bed within 3 hours than in those who waited 4 hours or more.

What it can't show

It shows that the two go together. People answered from memory, and it was one group in one city, so it can't show that waiting longer would change things for you. The range is wide too: "about 7 times" could be 3 or 16.

How to check your own evenings

You need two times (when dinner ended, when you lay down) and how the night went. After a few weeks, split your nights into shorter and longer gaps and count the bad ones on each side.

Watch for what comes along with it: late dinners are often bigger, richer, or after a longer day.

Source

  • Fujiwara Y et al. Association between dinner-to-bed time and gastro-esophageal reflux disease. Am J Gastroenterol, 2005
