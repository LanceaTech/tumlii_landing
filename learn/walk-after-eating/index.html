Does a walk after eating help your stomach?

Short answer. A slow walk can help the stomach empty a little sooner. In a study of 10 healthy men, half of a large meal had left the stomach after 107 minutes with a slow walk, against 123 minutes without. It made no difference to how full or bloated they felt.

What was tested

Ten healthy men ate a 576 kcal meal and had their stomachs watched by ultrasound on different days. On some days they had a small drink afterwards: water, espresso, or a spirit. On another day they had water and walked slowly (4 km an hour) on a treadmill.

After the mealHalf the meal had left after
Water123 minutes
Water and a slow walk107 minutes
Espresso125 minutes
Brandy119 minutes (no real difference)

So the after-dinner coffee or digestif did nothing for emptying. The walk did, by about a quarter of an hour.

What it can't show

Ten men without stomach trouble is a very small group, and their fullness and bloating didn't change. Whether a walk changes heartburn wasn't tested here at all.

How to check it yourself

Pick one meal, the one that bothers you most. For two weeks, take a slow 15 to 20 minute walk after it on some days and not on others, whatever you ate, and note how you felt two hours later both times. Then count. Keep it gentle: hard exercise soon after a meal is a different thing.

Source

  • Franke A et al. Postprandial walking but not consumption of alcoholic digestifs or espresso accelerates gastric emptying in healthy volunteers. J Gastrointestin Liver Dis, 2008
