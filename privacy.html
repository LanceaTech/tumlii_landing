Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Your diary stays on your phone. Tumlii's server only ever holds what you choose to share, and you can take it back.

Who we are

Tumlii is made by LanceaTech Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore ("we", "us"). We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012. If you're in the EU or the UK, we also aim to give you the rights the GDPR gives you. Questions or requests: privacy@tumlii.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you note down in Tumlii stays on your phone, in a locked (encrypted) store: meals, symptoms, sleep, stress, routines, recipes, Pregnancy Mode, your answers about allergies and food rules, and your name. We never receive it. If you save a backup file, it goes wherever you put it.

If you connect Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone), Tumlii reads sleep, workouts and weight on your phone. It never writes back, and that data never leaves your phone through Tumlii.

What reaches our server

  • Your account. We keep your account's email, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, a random account id, and which version of our terms you agreed to and when. Each phone you sign in on gets a secret key; we store only a scrambled copy of it. We never see your password itself, and we use your email only for account messages, like the code that confirms it's yours, a welcome, a password reset link or a deletion link, sent through our email provider (Resend). If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your Google account id, email and name; we keep the id and email to recognise you, and your name stays on your phone. We ask Google for nothing else.

And only if you choose:

  • "You're not alone" counts. Only if you say yes. We receive the names of dishes from Tumlii's list that your logs flagged, and nothing else: no dates, no amounts, no symptoms, and never foods you typed yourself. A count is only ever shown when at least 20 people share it, rounded down, so no one can be picked out. Turn it off in Profile and your contribution is deleted.
  • Recipes and routines you share. Only what the sharing screen shows you: a recipe's name, servings, ingredients, steps and your optional line; or a routine's times, your optional line, and rough figures (weeks used, days logged, how often you kept to it, the share of upset days before and during). Never your notes, diary or dates. They're public without your name. You can take them down at any time.
  • Reports you make about something shared, with the reason and any note you add.

Shared recipes and routines are checked automatically, and some are read by a person at Tumlii before they're published. Every publishing decision is logged.

Crash reports

If Tumlii crashes, the app sends a crash report so we can fix it: what went wrong in the app's code (the error and where in the code it happened), the app version, and your phone's make, model and Android version. It never includes your account, your email, your internet address or location, your diary or anything you typed, and no screenshots. Reports go to Sentry, which stores them for us in the United States for up to 90 days. You can turn crash reports off at any time in Profile → Crash reports.

Early access emails

If you leave your email on tumlii.com, it's kept by our website host (Netlify) so we can send you one email when Tumlii is ready for your phone. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Who helps us

  • Vultr hosts our server in Singapore.
  • Cloudflare passes traffic to it, which means it sees your internet address while a request is made. It also forwards mail sent to our tumlii.com addresses.
  • Netlify hosts tumlii.com and the early access form.
  • Google confirms who you are when you choose Continue with Google.
  • Resend sends our emails, such as sign-up codes and password reset links. It sees the email address and the message.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, if you leave them on (see above), and stores them in the United States.

We don't sell data or show ads, and the app has no analytics or tracking. The only report it sends on its own is a crash report, which you can turn off.

How long we keep things

What you share stays until you take it down, turn sharing off, or erase your account. Server logs that show internet addresses are kept for a short time to keep the service running and safe. The audit log of publishing decisions keeps ids and reasons, not your content.

Your choices and rights

  • See it: Profile lets you save everything on your phone as a file.
  • Stop sharing: turn off shared counts in Profile, or take down anything you shared.
  • Erase it: Profile → Erase everything deletes your Tumlii account and everything held for it on our server, and your diary from your phone. If our server can't be reached, nothing is erased and the app tells you. Without the app, use tumlii.com/delete.html.
  • You can also write to privacy@tumlii.com to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to complain. You can also complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, or to your local regulator.

Delete your Tumlii account

In the app: Profile → Erase everything. It deletes your diary from your phone and your account from our server, with everything you shared.

Without the app (for example, if you've uninstalled it): go to tumlii.com/delete.html and enter your account's email. We email you a link, and one tap on it deletes your account, your sign-in details and everything you shared, straight away. We email you once more to confirm it's done. Your diary was only ever on your phone, so we have no copy of it to delete.

Children

Tumlii is for people 13 and over. Under 18, alcohol is left out of suggestions. Under 13, please use Tumlii only with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and ask you to agree again in the app before it applies to you.
